{{ org_name }} uses Ingenium AI to manage recruitment and onboarding. This notice explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Account data: name, email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Recruitment data you provide: contact details, professional profile, resumes, applications and cover notes.
  • Assessment records created by our staff: screening notes, interview feedback and hiring decisions.
  • If you join us: employment, onboarding, training and probation records and the documents you upload.

We do not ask for, and our scoring does not use, age, gender, ethnicity, religion, disability, marital status or other protected characteristics.

How AI is used

Skill comparisons are rule-based and explainable. An optional language model running on our own infrastructure (Ollama) can summarise resume evidence and suggest interview questions. Contact details are removed before text is sent to the model. No hiring, rejection or promotion decision is ever made automatically — authorised staff review all evidence.

Access & security

Access is role-based: candidates only see their own records, interviewers only see what they need for assigned interviews. Files are stored privately and are never publicly accessible. Sensitive actions are recorded in an audit log.

Retention

Resumes of unsuccessful candidates are deleted after {{ retention_days }} days once all their applications are closed, unless you join us as an employee. You can withdraw open applications and delete resumes not attached to active applications at any time.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (for example India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 or the EU/UK GDPR) you may have rights to access, correct or erase your data and to withdraw consent. Contact our HR team to exercise them.
